The two-dot mark

Three ways to show two dots reconnecting. The left dot is the website (Report violet, the analysts' side); the right dot is the assistant that sent the visit (pink, the labs' side). Each concept is shown at favicon sizes, on every field colour, and in light and dark browser tabs.

A · Reconnected

Two dots joined by a short, heavy bar

The most literal reading: the link between the two is restored. Reads at 16px as a small dumbbell.

B · Bridge

Two dots with an arc reaching across

A link drawn as a bridge from one side to the other. Warmer and more gestural; the arc thins out at small sizes.

C · Overlap

Two dots that touch, the shared part drawn in ink

The two sides overlap and the place where they meet is drawn in ink: the connection is the thing both share. Partnership rather than a bridge; the most compact silhouette.
