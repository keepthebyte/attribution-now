For SEO, GEO and analytics teams

Your AI traffic is bigger than your reports show

ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude send people to your site every day. Many of those visits arrive with no referrer and no UTM tag, so your analytics file them under Direct, next to bookmarks and typed URLs. We test each assistant on web, mobile and desktop, so you know which of its visits you can see and which you can't.

For teams at AI labs

Small fixes, so every site can see the visits you send

When a link from your assistant arrives with no referrer or tag, the site can't give you credit. The fixes are small: send your origin as the referrer, and add one documented utm_source value. Each lab page shows what already works, what to fix next, and a prompt your team can paste into a coding assistant or a ticket.
